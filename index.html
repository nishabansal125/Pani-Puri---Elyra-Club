Pani Puri

Pani puri uses crisp hollow puris filled with ingredients such as potato, chickpeas and chutneys.

The spiced water often combines mint, coriander, tamarind, chilli and roasted cumin.

Another detail

Different regions use different names and fillings, so a pani puri plate can taste quite different from one city to another.
